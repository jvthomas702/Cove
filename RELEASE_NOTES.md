# Cove 1.7.0

**Your files, from the notch**
- The Files tab has a **Browse** view: Home, Desktop, Documents, Downloads, iCloud Drive and Applications, a path bar, sorting, and drag-out. Search filters the folder and, from two letters, searches your Mac with Spotlight.
- **Convert** images to a PDF (one page each, phone photos the right way up), combine images and PDFs into one PDF, change an image to JPEG, PNG, HEIC or TIFF, or export a PDF's pages as images. Nothing is overwritten, and the result lands on the shelf.

**Talk to your cloud agents**
- Pick **Cloud** in the AI tab to send a follow-up to a Claude session on claude.ai/code. Sessions behind pull requests Cove sees are listed for you; paste a link to add any other.
- **New task → Claude (cloud)** starts a cloud session for a project folder.
- AI tasks show their own titles, and cloud tasks get **Message** and **Open on claude.ai**.

**A new look**
- The music tile on Home is liquid glass: your album art glows through a frosted pane with round glass controls and a white play button.
- Buttons default to a matching **Glass** look (Outline, Glow and Tinted are still in Settings).
- A darker palette, and the open island is square at the top, flush with the screen edge like the notch.
- Everything moves on springs: the island stretches open, content comes into focus, lists build in row by row, tabs slide, and numbers roll. Reduce Motion turns it into simple fades.

**Smaller things**
- "1h left" instead of "1h 0m left", countdowns over an hour read "in 1h 15m", the Home due line follows your Due soon setting, and long tracks show hours.

Update through Settings → About → Check for Updates. Your existing Cove installation updates in place.
