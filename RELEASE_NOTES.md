## Install

1. Download **Cove.zip** below and double-click it.
2. Drag **Cove.app** into **Applications**.
3. Open it. macOS will say it can't verify the developer (Cove isn't notarized). Click **Done**, then go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway**.
4. Cove opens its settings on the Permissions page. Allow what you want to use, then use **Connections** to hook up Claude Code, Codex, the browser extension and the terminal tools.

Building from source instead (`./scripts/install.sh`) signs Cove with a local certificate, so macOS keeps its permissions across updates.
